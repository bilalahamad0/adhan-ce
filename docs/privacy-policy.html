Privacy Policy — Adhan Focus

Live prayer-time countdown with automatic cross-tab media pausing.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Adhan Focus is built to be privacy-friendly. It does not use analytics or trackers, does not require an account, and does not sell your data or share it for advertising or marketing. Your settings are stored on your own device. The only information sent off your device is: the location you choose (its city, region and country — and, on the welcome page shown after installation, its coordinates), with your calculation method and Asr school, sent to a prayer-times service; the text you type into the location search, sent to a place-search service; and — only if you click "Detect location" — a request to an IP-based location service (which sees your IP address, as any website does). It is used solely to look up prayer times and resolve places.

Who this applies to

This policy covers the Adhan Focus browser extension ("the extension"), listed as "Adhan Focus: Muslim Prayer Times & Auto-Pause" on the Chrome Web Store and Microsoft Edge Add-ons and under its earlier name, "Adhan Caster: Prayer Times", on Firefox Add-ons, and packaged for Opera Add-ons. It is a standalone extension, and this single policy applies identically to the Chrome, Firefox, Microsoft Edge and Opera builds.

Third-party services

To do its job, the extension makes requests to the external services below. The first two are used for its core features; the last is used only if you click "Detect location":

  • Prayer-times API (api.aladhan.com, the public AlAdhan API) — receives the city, region/state, and country you selected (plus your chosen calculation method and Asr school) in order to return that location's prayer times — once a day, whenever you change your location or calculation settings or press Refresh, and again shortly before each prayer to catch any correction. On the welcome page shown after installation, it also receives the latitude and longitude of the place you pick or detect (plus the calculation method) to preview that day's times. The extension adds no identifiers of its own.
  • Open-Meteo geocoding API (geocoding-api.open-meteo.com, see the Open-Meteo terms & privacy) — receives the text you type in the location search to return matching places and their coordinates.
  • IP-based location services (optional) (api.bigdatacloud.net, see the BigDataCloud privacy policy; and, only if that request fails or returns no usable location, ipapi.co, see the ipapi.co privacy policy) — contacted only when you click "Detect location". The extension sends no data of its own in this request; the service sees your IP address and returns an approximate city and coordinates. ipapi.co states that it keeps these queries in its web-server logs for a limited time.

As with any web request, these services automatically receive standard technical metadata (such as your IP address and request time). The extension itself does not attach any identifier to these requests. Data handling by these third parties is governed by their own policies.

What the extension does NOT do

  • It does not collect names, emails, passwords, or any personally identifying information.
  • It does not track your browsing history, and it does not read, store, or transmit the content of the pages you visit.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or fingerprinting.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone for marketing.

The extension's content script interacts with a page only to find <video> and <audio> elements so it can pause and resume them at prayer time. It does not inspect or send page content anywhere.

Data retention and deletion

All settings, schedule data and your prayer log live on your device. You can change your saved location and preferences at any time in the extension's settings and un-check any prayer in the tracker, or remove everything by uninstalling the extension (Chrome: chrome://extensions; Edge: edge://extensions; Opera: opera://extensions; Firefox: about:addons). The developer holds no server-side copy of your data.

Children's privacy

The extension is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from children.
